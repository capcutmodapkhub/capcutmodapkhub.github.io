Why aspect ratio matters

Every platform crops or letterboxes video that does not match its preferred ratio. A 16:9 landscape clip uploaded to TikTok gets shrunk into a small strip with black bars, while a 9:16 vertical clip on YouTube looks fine as a Short but awkward in a normal video player. Choosing the ratio before you start editing saves you from re-framing every shot later. In CapCut, set your canvas ratio first (in the project settings), then place your clips — text and faces should stay inside the safe zone, roughly the middle 80% of the frame, so platform buttons and captions never cover them.
