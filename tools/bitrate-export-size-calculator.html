What bitrate should you use?

Use caseResolutionSuggested bitrate
TikTok / Reels (vertical)1080 × 192012–20 Mbps
YouTube 1080p1920 × 108012–20 Mbps
YouTube 4K3840 × 216045–80 Mbps
Quick share / WhatsApp720p5–8 Mbps

Bitrate is the amount of data per second of video. Higher bitrate means more detail and less blockiness in fast movement, but also a bigger file and slower upload. Platforms re-compress your video anyway, so exporting far above their recommended bitrate mostly wastes storage. A good rule: 12–20 Mbps for 1080p social video, 45 Mbps for 4K. If your export looks blocky, raise the bitrate before raising the resolution.
