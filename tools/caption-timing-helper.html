How to use these timings in CapCut

CapCut's auto captions generate timings from your audio automatically, which is usually faster than typing them by hand. This helper is for when you plan captions before recording, or when you want to check how long your script will run. A natural speaking pace is about 130–160 words per minute. Keep captions to 2–6 words per line on vertical video so the text stays big and readable, and leave a small gap between lines so each caption has time to register. In CapCut, add your captions with the Text > Auto Captions tool, then adjust any line that starts too early or late by dragging its edges on the timeline.
