How to turn an idea into a finished video

An idea is only the start — the edit is where it becomes a video. Shoot or collect your clips first, then open CapCut and set the right canvas size (9:16 for TikTok and Reels). Place your hook clip first: the first two seconds decide whether someone keeps watching. Cut on the beat of your music, add captions in the style the idea suggests, and use one signature transition instead of a different effect on every cut. Export at 1080p, 30fps, 12–20 Mbps for social platforms. If the idea came from this generator, make it your own: change the hook line, swap the music, and film with your own clips — templates are a starting point, not a finished video.
