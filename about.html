About CapCut Guide

CapCut Guide is an independent informational website about the CapCut video editor — its features, templates, editing techniques and the honest facts about modified APK files.

What this site is

We publish original, practical guides for people who edit video on their phones: beginners learning their first cut, TikTok and Reels creators improving retention, and anyone wondering what CapCut Premium is actually worth. We also build free tools that run in your browser — an aspect ratio calculator, a bitrate and export size calculator, a caption timing helper and a template idea generator. Everything on this site is free to read and use, with no sign-up.

What this site is not

We are not affiliated with ByteDance, CapCut, TikTok, Google or Apple. We do not develop, distribute or host any app, and we do not provide download links to modified APK files. The only download links on this site point to the official CapCut app on Google Play and the App Store. Our guides explain what modified APKs are and why they are risky, because that question comes up constantly — and the honest answer matters more than the download.

How the guides are written

Every guide is written originally for this site, based on hands-on use of the official CapCut app. We test the steps we describe, we say plainly when a feature is premium-only, and we correct guides when the app changes. CapCut updates every few weeks, so a feature's location or name can move — if you find a step that no longer matches the app, please tell us through the contact page.
