Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026. This site collects almost nothing — here is the full picture.

What we collect

This website does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email or phone number, and does not run analytics that identify you. The tools on this site (aspect ratio calculator, bitrate calculator, caption timing helper, template idea generator) run entirely in your browser: anything you type into them stays on your device and is never sent to a server.

Cookies

This site does not set tracking cookies. Your browser may store ordinary technical data (such as cached pages) as part of normal web browsing, which we do not control and do not read.

Email contact

If you email us at contact@capcutmodapk.github.io, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use it only to reply to you. We do not share it, sell it, or add it to any list.

Links to other sites

This site links to Google Play and the App Store (for the official CapCut app) and to nothing else for downloads. Those stores have their own privacy policies, which apply when you visit them. We are not responsible for their practices.

Children

This site is a general information site and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page with a new date. Questions: contact@capcutmodapk.github.io.
